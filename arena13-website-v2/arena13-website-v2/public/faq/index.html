The essential things to know before your ARENA 13 visit.

Do I need to book online? +

Not at the moment. ARENA 13 is currently operating with walk-in ticketing. Online booking is planned as a future feature.

Where can I check the prices? +

Every activity page shows the current listed ticket price and the applicable unit, so you can plan your visit before arriving.

Are the activities suitable for children? +

Several activities are designed with families and kids in mind. Please check the individual activity information and follow the venue team's safety instructions before participating.

Can I visit with a group? +

Yes. ARENA 13 is designed for friends, families and group outings. For larger groups or celebrations, contact the venue team in advance.

Can I host a birthday or corporate outing? +

Yes. We can help you plan group experiences around the activities available at the venue. Contact the team for a current group arrangement.

Is online payment available? +

Online booking and prepayment are planned for a future release. For now, tickets are purchased at the venue.
